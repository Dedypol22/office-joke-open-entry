One line of office comedy for the "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open" bounty. The joke turns the most boring artifact in office life — the out-of-office reply — into a confession, and then lets HR take it at face value: "I set my out-of-office to 'I am out of the office and out of patience,' and HR asked which one returns Monday." The setup sounds like a normal auto-reply, the middle smuggles in the grumble, and the payoff lands on the last few words, when HR treats "out of patience" as a second, equally real absence with its own return date.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`.
